Fjør Tal is a roleplaying overhaul which adds a number of roleplaying and simulation features as well as other changes for more varied, challenging, and realistic gameplay.

Compatible with Skyrim Anniversary Edition Content, including Survival Mode.

Requires Skyrim Special Edition with Anniversary Edition Update.


*** Features ***

Slower Skill Advancement: (Toggle on/off with MCM)
Skills advance at 50% of the normal rate.

Curse of the Dead: (Toggle on/off with MCM)
Taking gold and other treasure from crypts may cause the player to incur the "Curse of the Dead". When cursed the player deals 50% damage to undead.

Drunkenness: (Toggle on/off with MCM)
New visual effects from drinking alcohol and skooma.

Gold has weight:
Each gold piece weighs 0.01 weight. Gold CAN BE DROPPED with a new power.

Backpacks slow movement:
Wearing a backpack reduces the player movement speed 20%

Gradual Encumbrance:
Movement speed is reduced at various levels of encumbrance. Inventory weight at 50% of carry weight causes a 10% movement speed reduction. This increases to 20% speed reduction at 75% of carry weight and 30% speed reduction at 90% of carry weight.


*** Immersion (Roleplaying Bonus) Features ***
(All Immersion Features configured with MCM Option - Roleplaying Bonus)

Immersion: 
New player stat that is gained through performing roleplaying actions. When full, the player is granted a bonus to Speechcraft skill advancement for 8 hours.

Sit and Talk:
Sit when talking to characters to have better chances to succeed with persuasion attempts, better prices with vendors, and earn extra Speechcraft XP from conversations.

Unencumbered Rest:
Rest while carrying less weight to wake with a higher Immersion value.

Daily Immersion Action: 
Doing any one of the following actions grants a bonus to Immersion until the player sleeps.
- Eating a food item
- Sitting for an hour or more (Game Time)
- Working at a crafting station for an hour or more (Game Time)

Silver Weapons Damage:
Silver weapons deal double damage against undead instead of a flat 20 point increase to offset added physical damage resistances to Draugr and Ghosts

Food and Hunger(Survival Mode) Rebalance:
The amounts of Hunger foods restore have been adjusted to better match the weight of the item. Several foods that had the "Very Small" Hunger restoration now have "Small" Hunger restoration. (You won't need to eat 100 raw cabbages just to be satisfied). Prices and weights of several foods have been adjusted to better match their in-word models and rarity. Several recipes have been updated to create multiple servings of the cooked food, so weight of the total ingredients more closely matches the weight of what is produced. There are also new recipes added to create already existing foods in different ways (ex: Make Apple Cabbage Stew with multiple apple types); To avoid making the food crafting lists too long, food recipes are filtered so you only see recipes for which you have at least one ingredient (excluding salt).

*** Animal Loot Overhaul ***

Animals, such as dear, no longer have treasure in their loot, but now have more meat and their full set of useful body parts.
Meat items and cooking recipes have been added for wolves, saber cats, bears, and more.


*** Enemy Poisons Overhaul ***

Enemy poison durations are greatly increased, but damaged is decreased.
Additional paralysis effect is added to some poison attacks.
Additional visual effects are added to some poison effects.


*** Enemy spells and inventory ***

Bandit equipment remains low level (Fur, Iron, Steel)
High level bandits use tempered versions of basic equipment rather than more valuable equipment.
Draugr bosses have new summoning abilities.
Silver Hand Weapons include tempered silver weapons
Thalmor use primarily Elven weapons
