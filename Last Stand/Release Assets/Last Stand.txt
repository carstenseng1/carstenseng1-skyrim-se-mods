Keep the most intense moments of action going with a valiant last stand!

When health becomes low, time slows and damage to the player is reduced in order to enable a Last Stand, extending the most intense action-packed moments of gameplay.


Requirements & Compatibility:

This mod requires only the base game and should be compatible with any other mods


Mod Settings (All Configurable with MCM)

25% Damage Reduction when health is at or below 50%
50% Damage Reduction when health is at or below 25%
Slow Time when hit when health is at or below 25%
